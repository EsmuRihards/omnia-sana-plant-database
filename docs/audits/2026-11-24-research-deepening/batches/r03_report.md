## Data quality fixes

**Dill:**
- A diabetes RCT and a lipid MA had been pasted onto five actions they do not state. Removed, and two real actions added: lipid-lowering and antidiabetic.
- The safety notes cited them plus a taxonomy page; those citations are removed.
- The allergy note named an uncited source ("Egyptian Drug Authority"); that attribution is removed.

**Burdock:**
- The only citation on the safety note was a taxonomy page. It is now rewritten from MSKCC (2025) and case reports covering atropine-contaminated tea, anaphylaxis, contact dermatitis, liver injury and pregnancy.
- Two unsourced claims were dropped: bur irritation and biliary obstruction.

**Andrographis:**
- New liver caution: a Thai ALT survey and COVID-trial enzyme rises, against a null real-world study.
- New info entry: adverse reactions concentrate in injectable andrographolide derivatives, not the herb taken by mouth.

## Not added (main reasons)

- **Combinations:** Kan Jang, ImmunoGuard, Feru-guard, Essiac, "Burdock complex".
- **Derivative injectables:** andrographolide sulfonate, Xiyanping.
- **Active-comparator-only trials:** dill vs mefenamic acid, gemfibrozil or chlorhexidine.
- **Publisher concern:** the marshmallow ACE-inhibitor cough RCT is in a journal from a publisher on Beall's list, so it is cited only through the review that includes it.
- **Near-miss conditions:** dill and postpartum haemorrhage; hollyhock compress and breast engorgement.
- **Angelica:** no new usable human study.
